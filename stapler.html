Precision Mechanisms & Product Development Case Study

Precision Mechanism & Multi-Part Assembly

Comprehensive 3D mechanical modeling, mechanical advantage lever kinematics, return spring stiffness sizing, and DFM injection molding tolerance verification.

Kinematic Linkage & Tolerancing Analysis:

Demonstrates our capabilities in consumer and industrial product mechanism engineering. The mechanical linkage transforms hand compression force through a multi-bar lever action into high-shear penetration pressure, incorporating plastic mold draft angles (1.5°), uniform wall thickness constraints, and spring fatigue cycle life estimation.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • Zero-interference kinematic motion study throughout full travel stroke.
  • Fatigue stress analysis on leaf and helical compression return springs.
  • Complete fabrication blueprint set including stamped sheet metal tooling and injection core/cavity details.

Classification: Multi-Part Precision Mechanism
Materials: Sheet Metal (SPCC) & ABS Polymer
