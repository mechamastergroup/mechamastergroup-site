Turbomachinery & CFD Optimization Case Study

Industrial Centrifugal Pump (Impeller & Volute)

Parametric 3D CAD modeling of backward-curved impeller vanes, drive shaft dynamics, and volute casing with multi-phase CFD simulation to mitigate cavitation.

Hydraulic Modeling & Flow Dynamics:

Engineered for demanding industrial applications including cooling water loops and chemical plant fluid handling. Blade curvature angles (Inlet β1 and Outlet β2) were iteratively optimized via ANSYS Fluent CFD to minimize boundary layer separation and turbulence losses while ensuring adequate Net Positive Suction Head Required (NPSHr).

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • Hydraulic efficiency exceeding 84% at the Best Efficiency Point (BEP).
  • Dynamic balancing of radial and axial thrust loads on rolling element bearings.
  • Fully dimensioned shop manufacturing drawings adhering to ISO 2858 and HI standards.

Technical Specifications

Configuration: Single-Stage End Suction
Flow Rate (Q): 120 m³/h
Total Head (H): 45 meters
Rotational Speed: 2950 RPM
Standards: ISO 2858 / HI Standards
