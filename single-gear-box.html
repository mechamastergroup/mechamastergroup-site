Power Transmission & AGMA Gear Design Case Study

High-Torque Single Gear Box Assembly

Helical gear tooth geometry optimization, shaft torsional-bending calculations, and bearing fatigue life verification adhering to AGMA 2001-D04 and ISO 6336 standards.

Gear Tooth Contact & Structural Stress Sizing:

Calculated tooth root bending stress (Lewis & AGMA formula) and surface pitting resistance (Hertzian contact stress). The helical angle (18°) was selected to achieve smooth progressive tooth engagement, lower operating acoustic noise, and balanced axial thrust loads across tapered roller bearings.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • High-durability case-hardened alloy steel (18CrNiMo7-6) gear specifications.
  • Ribbed cast iron casing (GG-25) engineered to resist deflection under maximum dynamic torque.
  • Complete manufacturing blueprint set including spline details, shaft fits, and oil seal recesses.

Technical Specifications

Reduction Ratio: 3.5 : 1
Gear Type: Single Helical Gear Pair
Transmitted Power: 15 kW @ 1450 RPM Input
Applicable Codes: AGMA 2001-D04 / ISO 6336
