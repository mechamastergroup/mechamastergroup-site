Kinematics & Multi-Body Dynamics Case Study

Radial Aircraft Engine Assembly

High-precision kinematic modeling of multi-piston motion, master connecting rod articulation, and high-speed dynamic tolerance stack-up analysis.

Kinematic Complexity & Motion Verification:

Radial engine configurations employ a single master connecting rod attached directly to the crankshaft journal, with secondary articulated rods pinned around its perimeter. This design required continuous multi-body dynamic simulation through full 360-degree rotation cycles to eliminate geometric collisions and verify joint clearances under high angular accelerations.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • Over 65 parametric components assembled with zero interference.
  • ISO 1101 GD&T tolerances applied to master rod knuckle pins and cylinder liners.
  • FEA dynamic inertial stress verification for high-RPM operation.

Technical Specifications

Mechanism Type: Radial Multi-Cylinder Assembly
Kinematics: Master & Articulated Rod System
Software Used: SolidWorks Motion & Kinematics
Drafting Standard: ISO 1101 GD&T
