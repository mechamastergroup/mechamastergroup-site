Industrial Material Handling & Structural Design

Industrial Incline Conveyor System

Full mechanical and structural engineering for automated industrial material handling, drive pulley sizing, and belt tension calculations per CEMA standards.

Mechanical Drive & Structural Framework Design:

Engineered for elevation transfer of bulk goods and manufactured packages in automated processing plants. The study analyzed incline friction resistance, cleated belt profile selection to prevent material rollback, take-up screw tensioning mechanism, and electric gearmotor torque requirements.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • Structural steel truss framework sized against dynamic deflection per AISC 360.
  • Integrated mechanical backstop anti-rollback clutch on head pulley shaft.
  • Complete 2D manufacturing blueprints for sheet metal side guides and welded truss members.

Technical Specifications

Incline Angle: 22 Degrees
Capacity: 120 Tons / Hour
Belt Width: 800 mm Cleated Belt
Design Standards: CEMA Belt Standards / AISC 360
