Bahrain World Trade Center (3D CAD Modeling)

A 3D CAD modeling showcase of the Bahrain World Trade Center, developed in SolidWorks with focus on geometric construction, dimensional definition, model organization, and structured parametric modeling.

3D CAD Modeling Scope & Technical Deliverables

This engineering showcase presents the development of a 3D CAD model of the Bahrain World Trade Center using SolidWorks. The work focuses on translating the building's distinctive twin-tower geometry into a structured three-dimensional CAD model while maintaining geometric consistency, dimensional relationships, and an organized modeling workflow.

The model demonstrates the application of mechanical CAD modeling techniques to a complex architectural structure, including feature development, geometric referencing, repeated elements, and overall model integration.

Key Engineering Highlights

  • Development of the Bahrain World Trade Center as a complete three-dimensional CAD model.
  • Reconstruction of the distinctive twin-tower geometry using SolidWorks modeling tools.
  • Application of parametric modeling principles to maintain controlled geometric and dimensional relationships.
  • Structured feature development and model organization for efficient modification and design management.
  • Integration of major geometric features into a coherent final 3D representation.
