Mega Project Case Study • MEP & Aerodynamics

Bahrain World Trade Center (MEP & Aerodynamics)

Comprehensive electromechanical (MEP) systems engineering, aerodynamic wind turbine airflow analysis, and district cooling integration for the 240-meter iconic twin commercial towers.

Engineering Scope & Technical Deliverables:

The Bahrain World Trade Center represents one of the world's most pioneering sustainable skyscrapers, integrating large-scale wind turbines directly between twin sail-shaped towers. Our engineering study encompassed central HVAC chilling plant load simulations, airflow balancing across the funneling architectural structure, and fire safety piping designed to international NFPA and ASHRAE standards.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • CFD aerodynamic airflow simulation through the central aerodynamic funnel, boosting wind turbine efficiency by ~15%.
  • Chilled water distribution network modeling for 50-story commercial office floor plates.
  • Full 3D BIM integration and clash detection preventing MEP installation interference on-site.

Centrifugal Pump — Mechanical Design Showcase

This engineering showcase presents the mechanical design and development of a centrifugal pump assembly, with emphasis on component geometry, assembly integration, manufacturability, and functional mechanical design.

The project includes the development of the main pump components, including the impeller, casing, shaft, inlet and outlet connections, and supporting mechanical elements, assembled into a complete 3D mechanical system.

Key engineering considerations include proper impeller positioning, shaft alignment, component clearances, flow-path geometry, assembly relationships, and practical manufacturing requirements.

The final model demonstrates a structured mechanical design workflow from individual component development to complete assembly integration and engineering documentation.

Engineering Scope

  • 3D CAD Modeling
  • Mechanical Component Design
  • Assembly Design & Integration
  • Impeller and Pump Casing Development
  • Shaft and Rotating Component Integration
  • Design for Manufacturing Considerations
  • Engineering Drawings & Documentation

Software: SolidWorks
