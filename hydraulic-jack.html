Hydraulics & High-Pressure Engineering Case Study

Heavy-Duty Hydraulic Bottle Jack

Precision hydraulic cylinder sizing, release valve mechanism, and high-pressure seal engineering designed for safe lifting of 20-ton industrial loads.

Hydraulic Sizing & Structural Safety Factors:

Based on Pascal's law of hydrostatic multiplication, manual lever pumping action is converted to hydraulic pressure within the reservoir base. Thick-walled cylinder hoop and longitudinal stresses were calculated via Lamé equations, paired with Euler-Johnson ram buckling verification under off-center loading.

Key Engineering Highlights:

  • Structural design safety factor (SF ≥ 3.0) guaranteeing zero rupture risk under proof load.
  • Integrated safety overload bypass valve preventing over-pressurization.
  • Precision GD&T machining tolerances on piston ground surfaces and polyurethane U-cup seal grooves.

Technical Specifications

Rated Capacity: 20 Metric Tons
Max Operating Pressure: 45 MPa (6500 PSI)
Hydraulic Stroke: 160 mm + 60 mm Screw Ext
Design Standards: ASME PALD / ISO 11530
